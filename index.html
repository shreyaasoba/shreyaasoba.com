Background

I did not write my first line of code until well into adulthood, and I am the first woman in my family to study abroad. Five years later I build production machine learning systems for a living.

That route shapes how I work. I am good at the unglamorous middle of a system — the parts where data is wrong in ways nobody documented, and where being careful matters more than being clever.
