What I work on

Most clinical data does not arrive clean. Lab names are free text invented by whoever configured the analyzer, records come through as scanned documents, and the same test carries different names across every system it passes through. Getting the mapping wrong does not throw an error — it quietly corrupts whatever is computed downstream.

My work sits in that gap:

  • Clinical terminology normalization — mapping free-text lab observations to standard codes
  • Retrieval systems over clinical terminologies and practice guidelines
  • Extraction pipelines that turn scanned clinical documents into structured records
  • Verification layers that let a pipeline decline rather than answer wrongly
